Title 9 - PUBLIC PLACES AND REGULATIONS  
Chapter 9.10 - SIDEWALK VENDING  
ARTICLE I. GENERAL PROVISIONS  

9.10.010 Purpose.

The purpose of this chapter is to protect public safety and the free use of public sidewalks by regulating sidewalk vending.

(Ord. 101 § 1, 2019).

9.10.020 Definitions.

A. "Applicant" means a person who files an application for a permit under this chapter.

B. "Director" means the director of community development.

C. "Hearing officer" means the city's administrative hearing officer.

D. "Permittee" means a person who holds a valid permit issued under this chapter.

E. "Sidewalk vendor" means a person who sells food or merchandise from a cart on a public sidewalk.

(Ord. 101 § 2, 2019).

9.10.030 Applicability.

This chapter does not apply to a certified farmers' market operating under Chapter 9.20.

(Ord. 101 § 3, 2019).

ARTICLE II. PERMITS  

9.10.040 Permit required.

No person shall operate as a sidewalk vendor without a valid permit issued under this chapter.

(Ord. 101 § 4, 2019).

9.10.050 Application.

A. An applicant for a sidewalk vending permit shall file a written application with the director on a form provided by the director.

B. The application shall be accompanied by the fee established by resolution of the council.

C. The director shall review each application for completeness within five business days after it is filed.

D. A certificate of insurance shall be submitted before a permit is issued.

(Ord. 101 § 5, 2019).

9.10.060 Issuance.

A. Within ten business days after an application is determined complete, the director shall issue or deny the permit.

B. The director shall deny the permit if the applicant has an unpaid fine under this chapter.

C. The director or the director's designee shall notify the applicant of a denial in writing, stating the reasons.

(Ord. 101 § 6, 2019).

9.10.070 Term and renewal.

A. A permit shall expire one year after the date of issuance.

B. The permittee shall renew the permit annually.

C. A permittee shall file a renewal application with the director at least thirty days before the permit expires.

D. The director shall act on a renewal application in the manner provided in Section 9.10.060.

(Ord. 101 § 7, 2019).

ARTICLE III. OPERATING STANDARDS  

9.10.080 Operating requirements.

A. A sidewalk vendor shall display the permit in a conspicuous place on the vending cart at all times while vending.

B. No sidewalk vendor shall vend within one hundred feet of an elementary school entrance during school hours.

C. Each vendor shall ensure compliance with all applicable health regulations.

D. A permittee may operate between the hours of 7:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m.

E. No vending cart shall exceed four feet in width.

(Ord. 101 § 8, 2019).

9.10.090 Prohibited streets.

A. The director may, by written order, designate streets on which sidewalk vending is prohibited.

B. No sidewalk vendor shall vend on a street designated under subsection A.

(Ord. 101 § 9, 2019).

9.10.100 Cart dimensions.

Vending carts shall not exceed five feet in width.

(Ord. 101 § 10, 2019).

9.10.110 Food vendors.

A sidewalk vendor who sells food shall obtain a health certificate from the county health department pursuant to Section 9.10.135 before vending.

(Ord. 101 § 11, 2019).

ARTICLE IV. ENFORCEMENT  

9.10.120 Violations and citations.

A. Any violation of this chapter is an infraction.

B. Upon observing a violation of this chapter, a code enforcement officer shall issue a written citation to the vendor.

C. A person who is issued a citation shall pay a fine of one hundred dollars within thirty days after the citation is issued.

(Ord. 101 § 12, 2019).

9.10.130 Appeals.

A. A person who receives a citation may appeal to the hearing officer within fifteen days after receipt of the citation.

B. The hearing officer may hold a hearing within thirty days after an appeal is filed.

C. The hearing officer may affirm, modify, or dismiss the citation.

D. If the hearing officer affirms the citation, the person shall pay the fine within ten days after the decision.

(Ord. 101 § 13, 2019).

9.10.140 Revocation.

A. The director may revoke a permit if the permittee has been cited for three or more violations within any twelve month period.

B. The director shall give written notice of revocation to the permittee.

C. A permittee shall surrender a revoked permit to the director upon receipt of the notice of revocation.

(Ord. 101 § 14, 2019).
